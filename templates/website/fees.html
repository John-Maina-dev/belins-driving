Statutory NTSA Government Fees

The following statutory charges are set by the National Transport and Safety Authority (NTSA) and paid directly via the eCitizen portal:

  • Provisional Driving License (PDL): KES 650 (paid on eCitizen)
  • NTSA Test Booking Fee: KES 1,050 (paid before test date)
  • Interim Driving License (3-year or Smart DL): As per NTSA gazette
